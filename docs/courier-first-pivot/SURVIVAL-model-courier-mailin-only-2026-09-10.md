**As currently shaped: no — it dies in November.** The Q4 valley (−£16–25k) exceeds the cash (£14.5k). See §4b for the corrected board.

**With the three valley bridges (Nancy stretch + Misha decision + rent conversation): yes.** The December–January run-rate is genuinely **+£8–11k/mo on a £29.7k cost base** with corporate at £7–10k, diagnostics growing, and the old co's £220k+ of creditor weight gone. **The effort question, honestly:** the next 90 days are the hardest the business will ever ask of you — CVL admin, corporate handholding, a website pivot, a supplier negotiation, and probably a wage conversation, all at once, for a business that pays you £0 until January. What you get on the other side is the clean, four-lane, diagnostics-spined company the old one never was. If that prize isn't worth 90 days of that pain, the honest alternative is the Safan+mail-in micro-shop (~£15k cost base, you two, no building) — profitable within weeks, small forever, and the corporate/courier/international ambitions die with it. Both are legitimate choices. The worst option is the middle: keep everyone, hope October is kind.

1. **The October joint.** The Nancy bill (£20–30k) and the corporate inflow (£7–10k/mo starting) are scheduled against each other, and the corporate side is handshake-only. If corporate slips a month, the cash hole is £20k+ with £14.5k in the bank. **Mitigation: confirm each corporate account's newco billing details in writing this month (email confirmation is enough at these ticket sizes), and stage the Nancy payment if any account wobbles.**
2. **Safan, still.** Every pound of diagnostics margin routes through one person at £3.5k/mo. In a clean newco this is now the single largest unmitigated risk — bigger than HMRC ever was, because there's no TTP for a person. Retention terms + the skill-transfer ladder (already defined in `skill-reproducibility.md`) are newco priority one after the pivot ships.

**What would change the verdict:**
- **Reopen "worth it":** corporate accounts materially don't move (only 1–2 convert), or the courier keep-rate lands pessimistic AND AdWords produces nothing by end-October → the shape is −£4k/mo with ~3 months runway, and the honest move is cut to a Safan+mail-in micro-shop (~£15k cost base) rather than wind down.
