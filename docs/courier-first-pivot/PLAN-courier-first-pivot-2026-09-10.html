Thursday 10 September 2026 · Prepared by Kimi from VPS evidence + the Sep 8 numbers pack · Awaiting Ricky sign-off 48-hour build scoped

1 The pivot at a glance

Walk-ins are breaking the team — ~17 unbooked drop-ins a week (21–27 in August) with no front-of-house since Naheed left. The fix: the website stops offering walk-in, sells courier collection across London with tiered pricing, keeps mail-in, and converts the people who still show up into booked drop-off slots — a path that already handles half our door traffic today.

Kill

Unbooked walk-in drop-off

~17/wk unbooked arrivals, each burning 10–20 min of a team that has no English-fluent front-of-house. Removed from the website, Typeform, and reception script.

Launch

London courier collection

Postcode → static price band → tier by repair value: free for high-value, half for mid, full price for low-margin. Booked automatically as a reviewed draft.

Keep & formalise

Booked drop-off slots

Already 16.8/wk — equal to unbooked. Becomes the only way to come to us in person: small capped slots, same/next day, team knows what's coming.

7 Same-day courier repair — 2027 research note

Your old courier-service playbook (same-day iPhone repair + return) is the right 2027 expansion — as an express MacBook-class product, once the team grows. All four of these must be true per job, and none exist as systems today:

① Parts in stock

Live stock truth gates the same-day promise. No stock → offer never shown.

② Engineer free

A named engineer with a real free block — capacity data, not vibes.

③ Slot booked

The repair is scheduled into the day — needs the D-09 capacity system.

④ Time proven

Average repair time per family, where even the slow tail fits the window.

Sequence: courier-first pivot → capacity/slot system → parts-stock truth → same-day pilot on one MacBook family (A2337/A2681 screens — price matrix and parts data already exist). Gophr priority-3 (~2× price) is the express-leg cost basis.
