What we repair on MacBooks in London

Tell us the fault in the quote tool and you get a fixed price. If we find more once it is on the bench, we send a written quote before doing extra work.

Cracked or dead screen

Genuine Apple display

Cracks, black patches, lines or a screen that flickers. We fit a genuine Apple display and test it before it goes back to you.

Liquid damage

£49 diagnostic

Spilt coffee, water or wine. We clean and test the board, then send a written quote within 1 working day. The £49 comes off your repair price if you go ahead.

Will not turn on or charge

Logic board repair

No power, no charge light or an unstable charging port. We trace the fault on the board and repair it at component level, instead of quoting a whole new board.

Battery, keyboard and trackpad

Genuine parts

Service Recommended warnings, fast battery drain, sticky keys or a dead trackpad. Fitted and tested in-house.

Flexgate and Dustgate

Micro-soldering

Stage-light glow along the bottom edge, or pink and grey bars on the screen. We repair the cause on the board where possible, without swapping the display.

Data recovery

Board and drive

If your MacBook is dead and the files matter, tell us first. Your original soldered SSD stays in place while we repair the board so your data can come back.
