Logic board and liquid damage

Water damage from a spilt drink, or it will not turn on or charge? We check the power rails under a stereo microscope, clean off the corrosion and replace the failed components on the board. Apple usually quotes a full logic board swap. We repair the board you already have.

£49 board diagnostic with a written quote in 1 working day, deducted if you go ahead. Board repairs from £249.

  1. 1Stereo microscope
  2. 2Damaged circuit path, repaired in-house
  3. 3Apple Silicon (original chip)
  4. 4Soldered SSD, kept on the original board

Data recovery

A full board swap usually means a new SSD and no files. We keep your soldered SSD on the original board and repair the damaged circuit path around it, so your files can come back. We prioritise your data whenever the SSD itself is undamaged. Severe liquid corrosion can still affect data, and we say so in your written quote.
