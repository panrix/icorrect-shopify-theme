Why iCorrect Is Different

We replace the display panel, not your whole lid.

Most repair chains and the Apple Store swap the entire lid assembly, throwing away your working aluminium shell, camera, hinge mechanism, and wireless antennas. Our microelectronics bench separates the assembly and fits a genuine Apple display panel into your original lid.

Genuine Apple displays, calibrated in-house

We reclaim and test genuine Apple panels and recalibrate colour accuracy, brightness uniformity, and True Tone so the finished screen matches factory output.

30% to 50% less than a full-lid replacement

Because you are not paying to replace an undamaged lid, hinges, and FaceTime camera, you get genuine Apple optical quality without paying for parts that are not broken.

Built for Liquid Retina XDR and M-series MacBooks

14-inch and 16-inch mini-LED MacBook Pro displays demand clean-room alignment and firmware calibration. We repair them daily alongside MacBook Air M1, M2, M3, and M4 models.
