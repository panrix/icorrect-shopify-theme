Is your MacBook screen cracked, flickering, or dark?

These are the display faults we repair most often on MacBook Air and MacBook Pro. We inspect flex cables, backlight rails, and hinges on intake so you only pay for the exact repair your MacBook needs.

Cracked glass or bleeding LCD

Panel replacement

Visible glass fractures, spiderweb cracks, spreading black ink spots, or dead bands after an impact or pressure on the lid. We replace the damaged display panel only with a genuine Apple display and keep your original aluminium lid.

Flickering, lines & Flexgate

Cable or panel

Stage-light glow along the bottom edge or a screen that cuts out when opened past a certain angle. Common on 2016 to 2018 MacBook Pro models (A1706, A1707, A1708) where the short backlight flex cable frays over time.

See Flexgate repair options

Dustgate bars & colour distortion

Micro-soldering

Pink or grey vertical bars, colour distortion, or a picture that only clears at specific lid angles. Caused by dust pressing into the hinge display signal cables; our microelectronics team repairs the damaged cable where possible without swapping the screen.

See Dustgate repair options

Dark or black backlight

Intake check

A faint picture visible under a torch, or a completely dark screen while the MacBook chimes, lights the keyboard, or works on an external monitor. We test backlight circuits and power rails first and quote clearly if a board repair is needed.
