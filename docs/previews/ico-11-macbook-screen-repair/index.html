Questions before you book

Ordered by the questions MacBook owners check most often before booking a screen repair.

Apple replaces the entire display lid assembly, including the aluminium casing, hinges, webcam, and wireless antennas, even when none of those parts are damaged. We replace only the broken display panel using a genuine Apple screen reclaimed and recalibrated in-house. Our repairs typically cost 30% to 50% less than a full-lid swap while delivering the same visual quality.
Yes. We fit genuine Apple displays reclaimed from genuine MacBooks and never use aftermarket copy panels. Every display is tested and recalibrated in-house so colour, brightness, and firmware compatibility match Apple factory standards.
Accidental screen damage is already outside standard Apple warranty cover. Our genuine Apple display repairs do not cause software conflicts, though Apple may decline future warranty claims once a third party has opened a device. Every iCorrect repair includes our own 2-year warranty.
Yes. Because we only install genuine Apple display panels and calibrate them in-house, your MacBook retains True Tone, full peak brightness, native resolution, and ambient light sensing.
Standard MacBook screen repairs take 2 working days once your MacBook reaches our Fitzrovia workshop (open Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm, closed Saturday and Sunday). That covers intake inspection, disassembly, genuine panel fitting, calibration, and final QC testing.
Collection is free on orders of £200 or more, which covers every MacBook screen repair. Same-day courier collection is available in selected London zones. Anywhere else in the UK, we send a free tracked, insured mail-in pack straight to your address.
We check power rails, backlight circuits, and flex cables before starting any screen fit. If we find logic-board or liquid damage, or a poor aftermarket display from a previous repair, we pause and send you a clear written quote before carrying out extra work.
Yes. We repair Liquid Retina XDR mini-LED displays on 14-inch and 16-inch MacBook Pro models (including M1, M2, M3, and M4 generations) using genuine Apple display panels and in-house calibration.
